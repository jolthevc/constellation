# Psychological Content Review Scorecard

Use before production, at editorial approval, and during postmortem. Score each dimension 1 to 5 using audience-specific evidence, not intuition alone.

| Dimension | Core question | Failure condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience relevance | Why would this person care at this exact viewing moment? | Generic topic with no audience reason |
| Scroll-stop clarity | Is the value or premise recognizable immediately? | Empty shock, meaningless movement or obscure setup |
| Curiosity / stakes | Is there an interesting question, conflict, or demonstrated benefit? | Manufactured mystery without substance |
| Retention progression | Does each beat justify continuing to watch? | Repetition, padding, bait without progress |
| Payoff and satisfaction | Does the outcome fulfill the opening promise? | Abrupt CTA or reveal withheld |
| Voice / identity fit | Is this unmistakably the property, not generic platform copy? | Trend copycat without distinctive perspective |
| Emotional fit | Does the emotional register serve the audience and story appropriately? | Exploiting fear, shame or vulnerability |
| Credibility / trust | Are claims accurate, represented honestly, and sourced where needed? | False evidence, deceptive synthetic media or unsupported claims |
| Sharing / saving motive | Would a viewer have a legitimate reason to save or share? | No usable, expressive or relational value |
| Return and follow incentive | Does this reinforce the ongoing value of the property? | One-off clickbait inconsistent with the brand |
| Production quality | Do sound, image, timing, pacing and accessibility meet the channel bar? | Distracting errors or robotic performance |
| Commercial integrity | Is any commerce relevant and clearly disclosed? | Fabricated testimonials or undisclosed incentives |

## Decision model

**Hard gates (no numeric average can override):** truthful claims, rights and permissions, synthetic media transparency where required, paid partnership disclosure, platform compliance, no manufactured testimonials, and minimum technical quality.

Use 1 = weak, 3 = acceptable but improvable, 5 = unusually compelling relative to the property's normal bar. Do not invent a universal score-to-virality prediction. Prioritize fixing weak audience relevance, weak payoff, or trust problems before optimizing transitions.

## Content brief requirements
Every brief should include:
- Audience segment ID and its evidence-backed psychological job.
- Property ID and its recurring psychological promise.
- Selected FMT IDs across presentation, story engine, visual treatment and platform surface.
- One-sentence viewer promise and why it matters now.
- First 3-5 seconds, next progression beats, tangible payoff and duration.
- Primary emotional register and how it is earned, not faked.
- Reason to watch, reason to share, reason to follow, and optional commercial fit.
- Source links, original contribution and permissions.
- Predicted metric and the specific experimental hypothesis.

## After publication
Record actual platform-specific observations: first-seconds retention, time watched, completion, saves, shares, follow conversion, returning viewers, qualified comments, production cost and any commercial events if available. Note missing metrics and confounders. Compare similar posts over time instead of claiming certainty from isolated successes.

## Audience versus property
If a content concept has high format fit but weak property fit, don't force it. Consider a separate property or modify the concept. If it has strong personality fit but weak audience relevance, return to research. If the content earns views without satisfied viewers, do not scale it blindly.

## Operational enforcement, not just a score
The governing standard is `../strategy/attention-retention-payoff-standard.md`. The canonical tactical libraries are `hook-library.csv` and `retention-and-payoff-playbook.md`. Every property MUST have an approved property-level `engagement-strategy.md`; every content item MUST have a brief modeled on `../properties/_template/content-brief.md` and pass `../operations/creative-approval-gate.md`.

Evaluate the exact **first visible frame**, the first **meaningful value delivered**, and the **specific payoff moment**. Consider no CTA a valid choice. When comparing performance, follow `../performance/engagement-testing.md`; claims that 1-second hooks, rewatch loops, or arbitrary jump-cut frequency necessarily improve ranking are not established universal facts.
