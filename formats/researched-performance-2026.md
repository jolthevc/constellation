# Content Format Evidence: What is Measured, What is Not
**Date:** 2026-10-28 | **Evidence type:** platform guidance + provider-specific observational study.

## Separating format layers
The original `catalog.csv` contains **149 composable format components**, not 149 independent performance categories. Every actual creative execution combines presentation, story engine, visual treatment, engagement mode and native surface. Provider studies compare broad *surfaces*, not all 149 archetypes.

## Measured macro findings

| Surface / question | Evidence | Defensible conclusion | Not established |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram Reel vs carousel engagement | Buffer 2026 median engagement per reach: 3.31% Reel, 6.90% carousel, 4.44% single image. [SRC-013] | Within selected Buffer-published IG posts, carousels had higher interaction rate per exposed account | That carousel causes more purchases, follower growth or original-recommendation lift |
| Instagram Reel discovery | Separate Buffer 2022-Oct 2024 dataset of 4M+ posts found Reels 1.36x carousel reach and 2.25x single-image reach. [SRC-013] | Reel exposure can differ substantially from interaction efficiency | Forecast for an unknown new account in Oct 2026 |
| TikTok videos vs photos/carousels | Buffer 2026 sample median engagement 3.39% video vs 1.92% photo/carousel. [SRC-013] | Video a plausible initial discovery test for TikTok | Universality across niches or causal video advantage |
| Instagram format segmentation | Metricool June 2026 examined ~24.3M posts from ~375K worldwide accounts. [SRC-022] | Multiple observational studies find material differences between Reels/carousels | Provider-agnostic benchmark due different samples/definitions |
| YouTube Shorts | YouTube says no format preference, uses chose-to-watch, duration, % viewed and satisfaction. [SRC-025] | Focus on first-frame relevance AND satisfying follow-through | Mandatory talking head, universal 1s cutoff or cuts every N seconds |
| Video podcast/audio | Edison 58% US ages 12+ monthly, 68% ages 35-54. [SRC-012] | Dual-format conversation has broad potential audience | Synthetic podcast will be trusted or watched |
| Originality on Instagram | Meta says 75% of US recommendations were original in Q4 2025. [SRC-020] | A replication-only strategy is high-risk; prioritize original editorial contribution | Guaranteed distribution for any original content |

## Design hypothesis matrix (NOT empirical ranking)
| Format combination | Audience job to test | Hook | Retention and payoff | Natural interaction | Risks / constraints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Faceless micro-doc + archive + vertical | Curiosity | Verified surprising fact | Narrative evidence then insight | Share or follow series | Rights, context and originality |
| Direct-to-camera POV + original humor | Identity/recognition | Relatable micro-frustration | Escalation then punchline | Share to friend | Awkward synthetic realism and forced cadence |
| Fictional podcast conversation + short video | Companionship/laughter | Natural high-stakes exchange | Real banter, callback and resolution | Rewatch/follow | Unnatural VO, false real interviews |
| Visual-first design carousel | Aesthetic inspiration | Strong first frame | Stepwise reveals, practical takeaway | Save | Generic style/stock image fatigue |
| Screen-recorded product or skill demo | Learning | Show real result | Authentic steps and limitations | Save/share | Fabricated product experience |
| Animated data explainer | Understanding | Concrete verified contradiction | Evidence and visualization | Share/save | Graph error and sterile visuals |
| Time-lapse craft/process | Sensory/awe | Beautiful work in motion | Tangible progress and finished result | Rewatch/save | Artificially staged output |
| Character sketch / 2D animation | Humor and attachment | Distinct world-specific joke | Escalation / recurring trait | Share/follow | Generic character, inconsistent world |
| Real street interview | Social surprise | Genuine original prompt and reaction | Actual spontaneous response | Share/discuss | Expensive location/consent, cannot fake |
| Photo story / archival essay | Nostalgia/context | Arresting authentic image | Precise backstory then resolution | Share/save | Licensing / factual attribution |
| Debate / community question | Belonging and choice | Meaningful tension | Strong case for both sides | Qualified comment | Rage bait and fake argument |
| Long-form investigative video | Immersion/mastery | Story and stakes in intro | Documented twists and clear conclusion | Longer watch/subscription | Much higher research/edit cost |

## Experimental principle
Do NOT optimize everything toward likes. Define one primary intended outcome per piece: qualified reach, full watch, follower conversion, saves, sends/shares, substantive comments or downstream attributable conversion. Capture secondary satisfaction/trust guardrails, then test creator-by-creator. See `performance/engagement-testing.md`.

## No universal production winner
An AI-generated talking head can be worse for perceived authenticity than an honest illustrated character or faceless expert explainer. A fake street interview is not a low-cost alternative to a real interview. A beautiful slow shot may outperform hyperactive cuts for visual mood. Format viability and native quality matter more than apparent automation capability.
