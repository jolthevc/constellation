# FMT-080: Honest product review
**Research stage:** macro_evidence_and_mechanism_hypotheses | **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 | **Not a validated performance playbook**

## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-015;SRC-013;SRC-019. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Purchase evaluation, avoiding regret and seeing independent evidence

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Show verified product behavior or precise tradeoff, not 'I swear by this' if the creator never used it

**Why they stay:** Feature demonstrations, price context, limitations and recommendation criteria

**What they receive:** Qualified honest verdict and who should not buy

**Natural intended response:** Save for decision; share with buyer. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. It solves one problem but creates another
2. The cheaper option wins only in this case
3. Here's the feature I would verify before buying

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Real purchase/testing, specs verification, commercial disclosure, accurate images. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Synthetic fictional 'first-hand' experience, hidden sponsor, unsubstantiated claims

## Experiment
Test demonstration-first vs comparison-first; track saves/reach and qualified clicks if authorized

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.

Sample at least 5 actual posts across 3 distinct reference properties, including typical or low-performing posts. Store public URLs, publication dates, exact observable first frames and payoffs in `../../research/datasets/creator-post-audit.csv`. Do not add invented retention or audience demographics. Upgrade to `researched_with_creator_examples` only when these are present, then to `internal_tested` after Constellation posts are measured.
