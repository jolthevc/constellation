# FMT-103: 2D illustrated recurring character host
**Research stage:** macro_evidence_and_mechanism_hypotheses | **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 | **Not a validated performance playbook**

## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-014;SRC-025. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Belonging, humor, familiarity, character world

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Distinct emotional expression or recognizably absurd character behavior

**Why they stay:** Recurring personality logic, visual motifs, escalating consequences or a useful topic explained with humor

**What they receive:** Real laugh, affectionate character beat or clear lesson

**Natural intended response:** Follows for returning world, shares for jokes. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. Our office mascot has one rule about Mondays
2. The character who always reads the fine print
3. This dog refuses to understand the dress code

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Consistent licensed character model, rig or illustration prompts, original scripts and voice acting. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Generic mascot without comedic personality, inconsistent look/voice or misleading human claims

## Experiment
Test character monologue vs two-character scene in same world; measure shares and repeat-follow

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
