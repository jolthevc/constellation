# FMT-135: Native short-form vertical video
**Research stage:** macro_evidence_and_mechanism_hypotheses | **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 | **Not a validated performance playbook**

## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-025;SRC-013;SRC-026. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Fast discovery across diverse subjects

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** First visible frame and early sound/text convey relevance with low friction

**Why they stay:** Concise natural narrative reward, excellent captioning, platform safe zones, scene duration chosen by meaning

**What they receive:** Specific emotional/functional reward

**Natural intended response:** Choose-to-view/retention then natural share/follow. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. A question answered by one object in frame
2. The exact instant the story changed
3. An unusual visual that explains itself

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
9:16 crop, sound design, mobile captions, editing and rights. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Overstimulation, arbitrary cutting, template clickbait, assumption one-second rule universal

## Experiment
Test first-frame variants with same concept when feasible, record viewed vs swiped, duration and satisfaction proxies

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.

Store public URLs, publication dates, exact observable first frames and payoffs in `../../research/datasets/creator-post-audit.csv`. Do not add invented retention or audience demographics. Upgrade to `researched_with_creator_examples` only when these are present, then to `internal_tested` after Constellation posts are measured.
