## Audience job hypothesis
Aesthetic reward, sensory immersion and completion

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Arresting authentic task and distinctive close-up movement

**Why they stay:** Visible action progress and well-paced tactile or sound feedback, no arbitrary overcuts

**What they receive:** Completed project and pleasant sensory resolution

**Natural intended response:** Rewatch for craft; save for inspiration. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. A perfect join with no voiceover needed
2. Watch the pattern resolve
3. From rough wood to the finished shape

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Real craft/action or clearly stylized animation, sound recording, durable visual continuity. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Unlicensed reused satisfying clips, fake result, generated continuity mistakes

## Experiment
Test natural audio vs restrained music and wide vs macro framing; track rewatches + negative confusion signals

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
