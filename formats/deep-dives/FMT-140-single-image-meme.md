# FMT-140: Single-image meme/editorial tile
**Research stage:** macro_evidence_and_mechanism_hypotheses | **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 | **Not a validated performance playbook**

## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-013;SRC-014. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Identity signaling, quick humor and social expression

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Recognizable visual-language plus immediately readable joke

**Why they stay:** No video duration; payoff contained in image/caption, optional second layer on reread

**What they receive:** Instant laugh or sharp insight

**Natural intended response:** Share to in-group, follow for consistent voice. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. A perfectly timed joke about the shopping cart dilemma
2. The one chart every coworker recognizes
3. A one-panel illustration of a universal awkward moment

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Original illustration/photography and on-brand language, not unlicensed repost. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Watermarked reposts, stale meme templates, joke unclear without insider context

## Experiment
Compare meme visual construction and caption specificity in a consistent series; track shares/reach

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
