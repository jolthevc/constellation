## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-025;SRC-013. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Clarity, mastery, specific surprising explanation

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** A verifiable number, map, diagram or comparison that communicates within a glance

**Why they stay:** Progressive visualization that makes causal relationships easier; short explanatory reveals and proper context

**What they receive:** An intuitive mental model the viewer can remember

**Natural intended response:** Save for reference or share for insight. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. This chart makes the housing tradeoff clear
2. The difference comes down to one overlooked denominator
3. Here's how the money actually moves

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Accurate data, charts, typography, code-render pipeline such as Remotion, mobile legibility. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Charts with misleading scales, decorative motion masking incorrect numbers, stale data

## Experiment
Test static-first versus reveal-animation openings using same sourced data, measure comprehension and saves

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
