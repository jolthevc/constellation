## Audience job hypothesis
Actionable skill and a clear solution

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Show the real end result or the specific failure, not generic '3 things everyone needs'

**Why they stay:** Visible sequential actions, accurate explanation of constraints and progress, occasional checkpoints

**What they receive:** An actually reproducible outcome or clear hire-a-pro decision

**Natural intended response:** Save for future use, share with decision partner. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. This repair takes two simple checks before replacing anything
2. Here is the setting that fixed the export quality
3. Watch this corner of the room as the layout changes

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.
