# FMT-149: Full video podcast with linked short clips
**Research stage:** macro_evidence_and_mechanism_hypotheses | **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 | **Not a validated performance playbook**

## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-012;SRC-014;SRC-025. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Deeper companionship or specialist insight plus discovery snippets

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** A full conversation with real reasons to return; snippets must be internally satisfying

**Why they stay:** A complete arc across an episode and self-contained clip payoffs that honestly represent it

**What they receive:** Episode-level learning or entertainment plus complete clip-level miniature answer

**Natural intended response:** Long watch, subscriptions, return visits. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. One argument that changed the episode
2. The question that made the guests rethink the premise
3. A short story that works even outside the full discussion

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Substantial original discussion, audio engineering, clip selection, character consistency. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Clip farms with no real show, invented guest interviews, excessive time cost

## Experiment
Measure full episode return/watches and independently each clip's discovery/subscribe behavior; do not attribute all views to same audience

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.

## Required next evidence
Sample at least 5 actual posts across 3 distinct reference properties, including typical or low-performing posts. Store public URLs, publication dates, exact observable first frames and payoffs in `../../research/datasets/creator-post-audit.csv`. Do not add invented retention or audience demographics. Upgrade to `researched_with_creator_examples` only when these are present, then to `internal_tested` after Constellation posts are measured.
