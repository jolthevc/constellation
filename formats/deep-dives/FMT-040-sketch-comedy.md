**Relevant sources:** SRC-014;SRC-025. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Recognition, surprise, shared culture and comic relief

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Instantly legible character/setting or an absurd collision of expectations

**Why they stay:** Escalation with real character logic, rising comic turns and surprise, not prolonged setup

**What they receive:** Punchline with closure or a clean callback

**Natural intended response:** Shares to friends and follows for characters. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. The person who takes a group project too seriously
2. A customer service agent who has a secret agenda
3. Every hobby has the same one guy

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Original writers, acting or expressive animation, efficient scene/voice continuity. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Derivative catchphrase borrowing, angry stereotypes, unfunny AI voice timing

## Experiment
Compare culturally specific cold opens vs visual absurdity; track share/reach and follow conversion

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
