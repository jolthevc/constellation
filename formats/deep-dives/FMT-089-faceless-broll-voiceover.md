## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-025;SRC-020. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Curiosity or utility without a human presenter

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Show the tangible surprise, source artifact or visual atmosphere immediately

**Why they stay:** Strong script with relevant licensed evidence; vary footage because meaning changes not to force rapid motion

**What they receive:** Insight or satisfying aesthetic/narrative resolution

**Natural intended response:** Share for real insight, follow for editorial taste. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. This receipt explains why an everyday product got expensive
2. The strange story behind this abandoned building
3. One ordinary decision caused an extraordinary outcome

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Clear source citations, original VO, B-roll licenses, editor directions, captions and pacing. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Generic stock montage with no original contribution, poor evidence and monotonous TTS

## Experiment
Compare archival-first vs designed graphic-first openings across similar stories; track hold, completion and cost per acceptable video

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
