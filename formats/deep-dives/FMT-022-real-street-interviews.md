# FMT-022: Real street interviews and vox pops
**Research stage:** macro_evidence_and_mechanism_hypotheses | **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 | **Not a validated performance playbook**

## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-025;SRC-019. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Social surprise, ordinary people's perspectives and rapport

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** An authentic engaging answer or fair question from an actually consenting person

**Why they stay:** Compact truthful interaction, context and contrasting answers; no fabricated spontaneity

**What they receive:** Human insight, warmth or genuine unexpected answer

**Natural intended response:** Share socially, comment with personal perspective. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. What small purchase actually changed your life?
2. What city habit surprised you after you moved here?
3. What advice do you wish you'd heard sooner?

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Actual filming, consent, releases, location rights, multiple takes and sensitive editorial review. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Fake AI-generated passers-by marketed as real interviews, undisclosed edits and coercive participation

## Experiment
Audit original live creator examples before investing; benchmark actual production time and safe footage quality

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
