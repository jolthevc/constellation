# FMT-002: Close-camera yap / commentary
**Research stage:** macro_evidence_and_mechanism_hypotheses | **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 | **Not a validated performance playbook**

## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-025;SRC-026. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Recognition, personality, timely thoughts, humor

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Specific opinion or recognizably true observation in first breath, without salesy template

**Why they stay:** Precise escalating examples, personal cadence, one or two emotional turns and a distinctive landing

**What they receive:** Recognition or laugh with unique voice

**Natural intended response:** Friends share to validate in-group experience. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. There's an unspoken rule in every group chat
2. Nobody warns you about the second day of moving
3. I have a theory about why these meetings never end

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Natural VO/actor, tight pacing, suitable close framing, believable reactions. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Robotic synthetic face, false first-person biography, recycled rants or rage bait

## Experiment
Compare specific observation vs direct question cold opens; monitor early holds and shares

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.

Store public URLs, publication dates, exact observable first frames and payoffs in `../../research/datasets/creator-post-audit.csv`. Do not add invented retention or audience demographics. Upgrade to `researched_with_creator_examples` only when these are present, then to `internal_tested` after Constellation posts are measured.
