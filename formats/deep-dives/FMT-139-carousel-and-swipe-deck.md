## Audience job hypothesis
Visual inspiration, saveable advice, comparative explainers

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** An arresting first slide with a clear visual or precise promise, not five vague introductory slides

**Why they stay:** Each slide adds a satisfying unit of meaning and visual novelty; final slide resolves or summarizes

**What they receive:** Coherent useful or beautiful full sequence

**Natural intended response:** Saves for reference, meaningful shares. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. Five small rooms that solved one hard layout problem
2. This design looks expensive: here's what actually makes it work
3. One simple map shows the difference

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Original/licensed imagery, type hierarchy, mobile legibility, slide architecture, captions. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Overtext, generic AI slop, unverified claims, inaccessible text contrast
