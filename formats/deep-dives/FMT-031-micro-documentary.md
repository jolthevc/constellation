# FMT-031: Micro-documentary narrative
**Research stage:** macro_evidence_and_mechanism_hypotheses | **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 | **Not a validated performance playbook**

## What external evidence establishes
**Relevant sources:** SRC-025;SRC-014;SRC-024. They establish platform reach, broad surface comparisons, podcast/fandom behavior or platform ranking guidance, depending on the source. **They do not establish a causal performance advantage for this particular FMT archetype, niche or generated-personality style.** See `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../formats/researched-performance-2026.md`.

## Audience job hypothesis
Broad curiosity or deep fandom

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Start with a specific verified surprising fact; not a category-generic 'you won't believe' setup

**Why they stay:** Establish a credible question; develop context, evidence and a genuine reversal; deliver an earned answer or honest unresolved uncertainty

**What they receive:** True surprise or new understanding

**Natural intended response:** Shared insight; longer series follow. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Three original opening approaches to explore
1. Here's the original document that changed this story
2. A tiny design decision transformed an entire industry
3. The famous explanation leaves out one crucial step

These are creative examples, **not** results or quotes from real competitor posts.

## Production and feasibility
Real research, editorial scripting, licensed footage, narration, graphics and fact check. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Reposting familiar stories, wrong facts, music/archival rights and generic fast VO

## Experiment
Test outcome-first vs evidence-first opens across comparable topics; measure chose-to-view, retention and qualified follows

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
