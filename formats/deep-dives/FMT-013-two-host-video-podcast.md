## Audience job hypothesis
Companionship, debate, comedy, insider talk

## Opening, progression, payoff
**Scroll-stop:** Begin at an engaging authentic conversational moment, no fake dead air or artificial shouting

**Why they stay:** Organic disagreement or accumulating insight, distinct character voices, responsive chemistry and a clear comedic/informational finish

**What they receive:** Punchline, perspective, insight or resolved argument

**Natural intended response:** Repeat watch and attachment to recurring dynamics. A CTA is optional and should never replace the payoff.

## Production and feasibility
Consistent lawful voices, believable turn-taking, facial sync if visible, editing, fictional disclosures. Estimate labor and model cost only after a pilot produces real quality-controlled examples.

## Known failure modes
Synthetic humans posed as real interviewed guests, wooden scripts, uniform voices, incoherent context

## Experiment
Test sound-first excerpts versus graphic-led audio clips; measure holds, completion, follow per view

**Primary outcome:** choose one of qualified view, avg watch duration, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, attributed follows or meaningful comments. Use native metric definitions, a consistent observation window and reasonable comparators. Record quality/confidence and production cost. See `../../performance/engagement-testing.md`.
