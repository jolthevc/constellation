## Psychological proposition
**For [specified audience], this property consistently provides [emotional / functional reward], through [distinctive perspective], unlike [alternatives].**

## Distinct point of view
What does this property understand or notice that alternatives do not? What editorial or aesthetic judgment gives it a recognizable personality?

## Emotional signature
Before watching: likely viewer state.
During: curiosity, warmth, awe, laughter, confidence, recognition, etc.
After: the promised positive payoff. Avoid claiming an emotion is guaranteed.

## Follow versus one-off view
Why watch one post? Why explicitly follow? Why return weekly? What does the property reliably deliver? What repeatable content series reinforces that promise?

## Relationship archetype and boundaries
Mentor, specialist, witty companion, explorer, critic, storyteller, peer, fictional host or institution.
Define warmth, intimacy, authority, humor, conflict, honesty, correction practices, synthetic identity transparency and parasocial boundaries.

## Identity alignment
How might following signal taste or identity? What in-group language is legitimate? Which assumptions could alienate audience subgroups?

## Voice and behavior
Tone and sentence rhythm, vocabulary, taste, humor, tension, pacing, recurring motifs, likes/dislikes, recognizable reactions, speech examples, boundaries. For AI personalities, link to voice/visual identity bible and approved assets.

## Trust and commerce
Evidence standards, editorial sourcing, sponsorship transparency, product fit, affiliate restrictions, no-go categories, audience feedback and commercial promises we will not violate.

## Test plan
Measure follow conversion per qualified view, repeat-view behavior, retention, saves, shares, meaningful replies, and relevant commerce outcomes. Keep a competing hypotheses log and avoid treating audience comments as representative of all followers.

## Change control
Do not have agents silently rewrite a property's foundational psychological promise in response to one viral post. Propose changes with evidence and owner approval.
