# Research an Audience
1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `audiences/README.md`, `psychology/framework.md`, `research/README.md` and the templates for SEG/INT profiles.
2. Confirm exact segment / interest under investigation, target region and platform where specified; don't invent other requirements.
3. Seek contemporary primary sources, sample distinct creators including ordinary/weak posts, and describe audience subgroups without stereotypes.
4. Record stop/stay/payoff/share/save/follow hypotheses, relevant FMT and HK options, commercial adjacencies and no-go risks.
5. Produce a referenced research brief and proposed profile revisions marked hypothesis unless tested.
6. Define falsifiable experiments and next-review date. Do not claim private audience metrics.
