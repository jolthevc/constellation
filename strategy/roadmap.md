# Phased Roadmap
Updated: 2026-10-28 | Milestones are evidence gates, not dates.

## Phase 0: Foundation (in progress)
Versioned strategic constitution, audience and format taxonomy, psychology, property template, graph structure, content workflow, quality gate, measurement contracts and agent instructions. No credentials in GitHub. No phantom properties or fabricated performance.

## Phase 1: Operator-led pilot
Choose one property and one primary social channel. Complete its identity, psychology and engagement strategy. Produce a small repeatable series, publish manually, log costs and results, conduct weekly review, and test at least two meaningful creative hypotheses without sacrificing quality.
**Exit gate:** consistent ability to create publishable content, enough comparable posts to distinguish likely patterns from anecdotes, known production cost and creator-specific identity that produces repeat interest. Do not invent numeric targets.

## Phase 2: Portfolio replicability
Launch a few deliberately different properties, ideally serving meaningfully different audiences, using common infrastructure. Begin to test whether shared intelligence and production workflows transfer. Keep property learning separate from global learning.
**Exit gate:** evidence of repeatable creative quality across properties and viable human workload.

## Phase 3: Assisted operations
Agents prepare research, generate creative candidates, assemble scripts/shot lists, review for quality, ingest metrics through approved APIs, and propose changes; human approves external action. Introduce storage/database when the volume and workflow require it.
**Exit gate:** documented reduction in hours/cost per acceptable post without a material decline in quality, trust or performance.

## Phase 4: Portfolio optimization
Graph-informed property investments, coverage measurement with uncertainty, multi-platform reach, commercial experimentation, resource allocation and property lifecycle governance. Bounded autonomous tasks with audit logs and rollback procedures.

## Change gates
- Major strategy, property identity, external posting, paid spend, account creation, commercial fulfillment: explicit human authorization.
- Routine research, drafts, internal analyses: allowed under documented workflows.
- Automation requires specific access, platform policy review, permission boundaries and measurable evaluations.
