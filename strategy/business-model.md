# Commercial Architecture
Status: hypotheses, not forecasts | 2026-10-28

## Core thesis
An authentic audience relationship provides permission to recommend or distribute relevant products and services. Monetization is downstream of trusted attention; the network may eventually be both an advertising medium and a launching pad for owned products.

## Revenue families
| Model | Entry condition | Unit | Potential failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform monetization | Eligibility, original content, platform rules | RPM or revenue per content | Dependency, low payout, policy shifts |
| Sponsorships / integrations | Demonstrated real audience fit and brand safety | Net fee per deliverable | Sponsorship overload, weak trust |
| Affiliate / referral commerce | Truthful specific product fit and disclosures | Net contribution per attributable purchase | Attribution gaps, low conversion |
| Licensed media / brand services | Rights, repeatable quality, buyer demand | Contract gross profit | Service-heavy overhead |
| Owned products / brands | Specific audience demand and validated economics | Contribution per order | Inventory, support, returns, capital |
| Audience-owned distribution | Explicit opt-in and genuine utility | Subscriber lifetime value | Consent, unsubscribes, privacy |

## Decision tree
1. Is there meaningful audience value and reliable content performance?
2. Is the audience actually commercially relevant to the proposed category?
3. Can the property introduce commerce without breaking its relationship promise?
4. Is the offer truthful, allowable, and provably useful?
5. Is contribution margin positive after creative cost, fulfillment, platform fees and support?

## Standard unit economics
- Contribution per post = attributable net revenue minus variable creation/promotion/fulfillment cost, with attribution uncertainty shown.
- Property contribution = net earned revenue minus all attributable costs; separate shared overhead, sunk R&D and fixed management time.
- Revenue per 1,000 relevant views only when definitions comparable; do not substitute raw views for monetizable audience.
- Evaluate owned-product launches on incrementality, repeat purchase, returns and acquired customer profitability, not simply free-media assumption.

## Commercial classification
Shared categories live in `../audiences/commercial-categories.yaml`. Property-specific permissions and no-go categories belong in `properties/<id>/monetization.md`.

## Initial pilot
Do not build sales operations first. Capture the audience-to-commercial-category hypothesis and any monetization experiments while validating creative quality and retention.
