# Portfolio Opportunity and Capital Allocation
Status: proposed decision framework, **not** a validated forecasting model.

## Score candidate property concepts on
1. Audience opportunity: identifiable community, relevant need and genuine interest.
2. Creative viability: repeatable, distinctive, strong hooks/progression/payoffs, not merely low-cost output.
3. Network incrementality: useful new audience/context, not just another account targeting the same segment.
4. Production feasibility: rights, tooling, visual consistency, time and cost at the quality floor.
5. Commercial adjacency: potential partners/categories that the audience and property genuinely fit.
6. Operational durability: sustainable topic backlog, voice consistency, community management and platform diversification.

Record scores with their evidence quality; weighting should be calibrated only after multiple pilots. A high total never overrides a failure of truth, rights, creator quality, or trust.

## Launch memo must answer
- Why this audience and why now?
- Which unmet psychological job and recurring media reward?
- Who already serves them, with cited examples?
- What is the unique property thesis and fit with existing graph?
- Which three viable format strategies and what will it cost to test them?
- What would cause us to kill, revise, or expand the experiment?
- Who approves launch, content, publishing and spend?

## Portfolio review
Monthly: review differentiated reach and production economics; separate platform effects and exogenous trends from genuine creative learning. Avoid launching ten similar accounts solely because one worked. See `../graph/model.md` and `../performance/portfolio-reviews/_template.md`.
