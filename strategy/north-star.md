# Constellation: North Star
Status: working strategic constitution | 2026-10-28

## Mission
Build an AI-native network of distinct, credible media properties that can repeatedly earn attention across a broad spectrum of interests, life stages, communities and consumer needs. Each property should be useful and desirable on its own; the portfolio's unique asset is differentiated audience distribution and the knowledge required to build it.

## The ambition
Reach audiences in multiple legitimate contexts, with a portfolio of characters, editorial brands, faceless publishers and other formats. Individual properties can range from niche to very large. Do not optimize for a particular number of accounts, a synthetic-human default or raw summed follower counts.

## What we actually own
- Property identities, creative systems, editorial processes, audience relationships and the content/rights we control.
- Accumulated learnings on audience motivations, formats, production economics and commercial fit.
- Measurement methods and strategic portfolio relationships.
We **do not** own the recommendation algorithms, platform users' identities, or guaranteed impressions. Portable owned touchpoints and platform diversification may reduce dependence over time.

## Objective hierarchy
1. Audience value, credibility, safety and creative originality.
2. Repeatably compelling attention and fulfilled viewer expectations.
3. Differentiated reach, relevant depth, retention, return and incremental portfolio coverage.
4. Reliable economics: content cost, production capacity, revenue potential and opportunity cost.
5. Increasing automation only after human-confirmed quality and repeatability.

## The graph thesis
A portfolio of independent audience nodes can cover more relevant communities than one generic mass account. Overlaps may support frequency, but duplicates do not automatically equal extra unique reach. Graph edge strengths must be evidence-backed and uncertainty recorded.

## What is not the project
Not a clickfarm, purchased-engagement network, deceptive identity/impersonation system, a factory of generic AI talking heads, a media-buying arbitrage scheme, or a collection of unfocused trend-chasing feeds.

## Operating sequence
One property operated personally -> multiple repeatable formats and channels -> measured portfolio coverage -> assisted production and research -> bounded agent autonomy. See `roadmap.md`.

## Questions we deliberately leave open
Which property first, platforms, concrete production stack, creative genre, capacity, monetization, budget and acceptable payback period. These are to be determined by pilots, not fabricated upfront.
