# Controlled Terminology
**Property (PROP):** enduring media brand/editorial account/fictional creator concept; may have multiple social channels.
**Channel (CHAN):** actual account on a specific platform.
**Audience segment (SEG):** hypothesis-backed population defined by a combination of behaviors, needs, circumstances and demographics. Not mutually exclusive.
**Interest (INT):** theme/community/taste that can intersect many segments.
**Format (FMT):** reusable component or publishing surface from the five-layer format catalog.
**Hook (HK):** opening pattern to create qualified attention, linked to a truthful promise.
**Content item (CNT):** an individual post or media artifact, including variants.
**Experiment (EXP):** a recorded hypothesis, comparator, metric and outcome.
**Research source (SRC):** URL and evidence provenance for a claim.
**Coverage edge:** supported link between property/channel and audience/interest; strength and source are required.
**Overlap:** estimated or measured shared reach among two channels; method and uncertainty required.
**Incremental reach:** additional *deduplicated* people exposed, not sum of followers or impressions.
**Qualified attention:** views from relevant audiences with meaningful watch/engagement quality.
**Payoff:** actual reward given by the content, e.g. laugh, answer, practical application or aesthetic delight.
**Native surface:** platform-specific publishing type (short, carousel, live, podcast, etc.).
**Production feasibility:** ability to repeatedly achieve a defined quality bar at acceptable cost.
**Status labels:** proposed, researching, pilot, active, paused, retired; use these consistently.
**Evidence labels:** hypothesis, secondary_report, primary_official, direct_observation, internal_test, internal_measured.
