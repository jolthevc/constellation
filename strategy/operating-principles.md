# Operating Principles
Version 1.0 | 2026-10-28

1. **Entertainment or genuine utility first.** Content must be worth watching on its own. Exceptional hook, meaningful progression and satisfactory payoff are mandatory creative concerns; implementation varies by property.
2. **Native to the audience.** Use observed audience needs, culture, platform context and experiments. Avoid demographic stereotypes or universal psychological claims.
3. **Differentiated properties.** Each brand has a recognizable and defensible voice, format grammar, relationship promise and reason to follow.
4. **Evidence grades.** Label observations, external research, hypotheses, deliberate choices, and measured internal results. Cite links and review dates for dynamic external claims.
5. **Research before imitation.** Study creators and formats, derive original mechanisms, never reproduce distinctive scripts, identities or misleadingly emulate real people.
6. **Human oversight at pilot stage.** Humans approve property launches, major identity changes, external publication, spending and consequential commercial promises.
7. **Honest synthetic media.** Do not fabricate firsthand product experiences, interviews, locations, credentials, audiences or testimonials. Respect consent, disclosure, IP and platform rules.
8. **No vanity metric trap.** Reach, watch time, genuine interactions, satisfaction, follow conversion, incremental audience coverage and unit economics must be considered together.
9. **Low operational drag.** Reusable templates and stable entity IDs; avoid unnecessary infrastructure before a measured need emerges.
10. **Cost-aware media.** Format suitability, human editing time, visual consistency, production cost and rights determine feasibility, not novelty alone.
11. **Learning is the asset.** Every experiment must generate a revisitable observation or decision. Failed posts yield useful data when recorded accurately.
12. **No forced gimmicks.** Never require artificial suspense, repetitive engagement bait, fake urgency, random cuts or obligatory CTAs.

See `attention-retention-payoff-standard.md` for the mandatory creative standard and `../operations/compliance.md` for trust boundaries.
