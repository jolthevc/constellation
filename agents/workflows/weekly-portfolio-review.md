# Workflow: Weekly Property and Portfolio Learning
1. Pull or manually enter consistent dated analytics for real channels, with platform source definitions.
2. Compare post types, duration and exposure; map opening hold, retention, completion, payoff feedback, social actions and follower conversion.
3. Flag evidence gaps, suspicious apparent anomalies, rights issues and potential trust erosion.
4. Summarize the highest-confidence and most important uncertain findings, linked to content/experiment IDs.
5. Propose next tests, format mix and improvements; do NOT independently change identity or production spend.
6. Update `properties/<id>/learnings.md`, `operations/weekly-review.md` (filled instance), and portfolio monthly review as appropriate.
7. Update graph coverage/overlap ONLY with legitimate data and methodology.
Exit: specific next experiments and owner approvals, not a vague dashboard report.
