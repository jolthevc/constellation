# Workflow: Idea -> Publication -> Learning
Trigger: approved property idea.
1. Validate audience fit, identity, commercial constraints, FMT/HK IDs and specific viewer payoff.
2. Produce content brief with alternative hooks where worthwhile and verify research sources.
3. Script, design/record/render, build production manifest and prepare caption/title/platform metadata.
4. Independent quality gate checks promise, progression, payoff, voice, authenticity, rights, disclosure and CTA feasibility.
5. Human explicitly approves content and publication settings. Pending or failed review means no post.
6. Publish through a manual approved channel, or later through a separately authorized platform integration.
7. Record actual post URL/ID, analytics and cost in portfolio tables.
8. Conduct postmortem; update property learning and shared format/psychology only with measured evidence.
Exit: complete content package and review, never just a generated video.
