# Workflow: Research -> Proposed Property
Trigger: human asks to evaluate an audience / commercial opportunity.
1. Read strategy, graph and audience taxonomy.
2. Research audience jobs with sources; examine at least several real alternatives, formats and negative examples.
3. Estimate creative viability honestly; propose viable identities and formats, including faceless/editorial as appropriate.
4. Describe hook-retention-payoff relationship, follow reason, commercial adjacency, original differentiation and costs.
5. Create a *proposal* using `strategy/portfolio-allocation.md` and candidate PROP ID marked unapproved.
6. Human decides launch / research more / reject.
7. If approved, copy the property template, allocate stable IDs, update registry, add planning graph edges as hypothesis.
Exit: approved property bible, first series and budget/quality constraints. No external account creation without separate authorization.
