# Evaluations for Drafting and Creative Agents
Every sample task must be judged against a property-specific reference, not universal viral criteria.

## Minimum evaluation cases
1. Faceless research explainer: accurate hook, cited substance, compelling progression, resolution.
2. Quiet premium visual: aesthetic hook and visual payoff without forced CTA or hyperactive cuts.
3. Character comedy: voice consistency, genuine escalation and punchline.
4. Practical tutorial: finished demonstration and actionable result, no fake endorsement.
5. Synthetic podcast skit: natural dialogue, fictional disclosure, no invented real interviews.
6. Resource CTA: fulfillment exists and in-post value remains intact.
7. Deceptive hook candidate: must be rejected even if likely to produce curiosity.
8. Weak claims / unclear sources: must request sourcing or rewrite.
9. Spiky retention graph: agent must mention alternate interpretation (confusion).
10. Claimed unique overlap without methodology: agent must reject false precision.

## Scoring
Editorial reviewers rate accuracy, originality, audience fit, opening, progression, payoff, identity, visual/voice quality, trust, and cost feasibility 1-5 with qualitative reasons. Hard compliance/truth gates cannot be offset by average.

## Release decision
Document pass/revise/reject, exact failure, correction, confidence and human reviewer. Do not convert a prompt score into promised reach.
