## Most important strategic inference
Research should not force us to launch five faceless documentary accounts simply because the scripts are cheap. Constellation's value depends on *different reasons to follow* and meaningful demographic/interest coverage. An identical creative engine can create overlapping but nonincremental audiences. Conversely, launching very different properties can make measurement of production economics and overlap harder. Pilot deliberately diverse creative grammars, one at a time.

## Hypothesis-to-test selection
**Option A, reach-first:** 30-60s originally reported curiosity videos. First-frame source artifact -> story progression -> documented payoff. Use qualified organic reach and follows per comparable view; production time and rights are hard constraints.

**Option B, commerce-ready utility:** home maintenance/design visual guides using actual licensed material. Result-first -> verified useful steps -> save-worthy guide. Save rate and practical viewer feedback primary; conversion only if real affiliate/sponsor systems available.

**Option C, personality compounding:** recurring illustrated comic identity or fictional conversational series. In-group recognition -> escalation -> punchline/relief. Genuine shares and returning viewer signals primary; no invented human biography.

## How to resolve shortlist
For each shortlisted category:
1. Complete public audit of 8-12 differentiated accounts, sample typical and weak posts as well as strong ones.
2. Generate 3-5 original property candidates with niche-specific psychological promise; define why they deserve recurring attention.
3. Estimate actual per-post cost from **one produced quality-approved sample**, not a hypothetical model RPM.
4. Test multiple original concepts at matched quality, duration and distribution surface; gather real view/drop/share/follow evidence.
5. Evaluate after comparable samples, then choose continue/iterate/kill. A single viral post does not prove property fit.
6. Require all claims, rights, synthetic disclosure and age-related safeguards.

## What could reverse the rankings
High real footage / legal costs, inability to achieve original narrative substance, low trust in synthetic hosts, account saturation and poor audience incrementality can move a promising category down. Unexpected strong hobby community and low edit cost could move niche formats up. No locked numerical opportunity scores are appropriate yet.
