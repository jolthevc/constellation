# Evidence Datasets (2026-10-28)

These are small transcriptions from published reports, not scraping, API-derived account metrics or a causal model.

- `platform-adoption-us-2026.csv`: Pew 2026 US adult **ever-use** by age and gender, in percentage points, source `SRC-011`. Do not use as daily use, video consumption rate, commercial buyer count or unique incremental graph reach.
- `evidence-points-2026.csv`: high-value, single-point observations from Pew, Edison, Deloitte, Buffer, IAB, AARP, HIRI, Houzz, ESA, Meta and GWI. Each has population, measure, period, source and limitation.

**Denominator discipline:** 58% of US ages 12+ consuming podcast monthly is not comparable with 68% of ages 35-54 unless those populations and time windows are explicit. Social media use by U.S. adults differs from teen user reason surveys. Buffer per-reach engagement differs from follower-based engagement. AARP 50+ weekly streaming differs from Pew 65+ ever using YouTube. Houzz users aren't all homeowners. IAB forecasts are not realized revenue.

**Methodology:** See `../sources.csv` for sample frames, publishers and URL, and `../2026-evidence-synthesis.md` for interpretation. All source numbers copied at report rounding precision. For any new metric add exact source, date, population, denominator, period and caveats. Research statistics do NOT replace first-party property performance.
