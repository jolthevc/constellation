# Trend Research
Short-lived observations belong here with an observed date, URL, expected decay window, account/format examples and uncertainty.

Do not treat a trend as a permanent audience preference, clone a creator's distinctive joke/identity or launch a new property solely because one sound/effect spiked. Prefer identifying the underlying psychological and storytelling mechanism and whether it can be translated into original work.

Every trend brief must name which property can authentically use it, likely audience reward, production difficulty, disclosure/rights risks and when to re-evaluate.
