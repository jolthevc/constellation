# Creator / Format Forensic Research Protocol
Status: executable human/agent research design, **not yet a completed post-level audit**.
Date: 2026-10-28

## Critical missing evidence
Broad surveys tell us which platforms people use, and provider-level studies compare formats, but **not** whether a particular avatar style, persona voice or specific hook will perform in our target niche. Public creator benchmarking is the next validation layer.

## Minimum audit per priority niche
- Select 8-12 unrelated, currently active reference accounts across three tiers: major, mid-sized and niche. Favor originality and distinct voices, not lookalike content.
- Sample at least 6-10 recent posts/account, systematically selected within a declared date range. Include low and typical performers, **not only top viral examples**.
- Record **per post**: URL, access/publish date, platform, format IDs, public likes/views/comments if visible, views per follower where meaningful, upload age, hook first frame/first spoken line, topic, emotional job, progression, final payoff, CTA, production method, source rights.
- Record **per account**: position, whether real/faceless/illustrated/AI, audience hypothesis, consistency, recurring series, apparent production demand, legal/misrepresentation risk, original contribution.
- Mark private analytics as `unavailable`. No invented watch time, retention, follower demographics or saves if not public.
- Convert into matched creative experiments at a controlled pilot property: original hook variants, comparable duration/topic, accurate viewer promise and meaningful payoff.

## Sampling caveats
Public view counts are affected by post age, followers, platform, exposure, topic and paid amplification. Views per follower is not a clean efficiency metric when nonfollowers drive exposure. Do not infer causal power from one successful post or failed peer.

## Code-ready CSV contract
`research/datasets/creator-post-audit.csv` begins with header only. Each row requires actual public URL, date, observed metric and source. Do not put sample placeholders masquerading as observed posts.

## High-priority audits
1. Faceless curiosity explainer: original script, archival rights and insight.
2. Comedic character identity: synthetic human versus illustrated representation; dialogue/VO quality.
3. Home maintenance tutorial / real result: commercial utility and source/demo requirements.
4. Premium aesthetic carousel: why share/save vs no-CTA video.
5. Podcast-style conversation: compelling exchange without fake authenticity.
6. Gaming/hobby fandom: in-group knowledge, recurring beats, social rewatch.
7. Older-adult practical information: pace, clarity, trust signals and age-appropriate but non-patronizing voice.

## Research deliverable
For each audit write the evidence table, links, negative examples, original angle, hypothesis, feasibility, test and stop criteria. Research conclusions should be tagged `observational_public` until an internally measured test exists.

## Review rules
Some social platforms block automated collection or restrict scraping. Use permitted public access and compliant APIs, no credential circumvention or harvesting follower identities. Rights and fair use require individual review.
