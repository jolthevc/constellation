# Non-negotiable Engagement and Payoff Quality Gate
Status: required before publication; human gate during manual pilot.
Read: `strategy/attention-retention-payoff-standard.md`, `psychology/content-scorecard.md`, target property's `engagement-strategy.md`, and its individual content brief.

## Hard fails: do not publish until fixed
- Opening promises a result, answer, exclusive access or experience that the content does not provide.
- Viewer is coerced, misled, or sent to a comment/DM funnel to obtain the only promised payoff.
- Generated personality falsely presents an invented lived experience or product use as authentic; synthetic interviewing/reporting is presented as real.
- False testimonials, fake engagement, illegal or undisclosed paid promotion, unlicensed likeness/voice, rights violations.
- Substantive claims lack necessary evidence; important context has been omitted to manufacture outrage.
- AI VO or visuals are jarringly unnatural for the target property; broken continuity or unreadable captions.
- CTA resource doesn't exist, cannot be delivered, or has unreviewed privacy/consent issues.

## Creative review (1 weak, 3 serviceable, 5 exceptional; compare to property's own bar)
1. Qualified scroll-stop: immediately understandable and interesting to the intended viewer.
2. Hook credibility: interesting but does not misrepresent the actual value.
3. First meaningful reward: early viewer progress, not just prolonged teasing.
4. Retention progression: each beat changes something; no obvious padding.
5. Payoff satisfaction: promised insight, laugh, utility, feeling or result actually lands.
6. Native property voice: distinctly this brand rather than generic creator copy.
7. Sharing/saving/interaction potential: naturally earned and optional.
8. Repeat relationship: reinforces what followers can expect in the future.
9. Originality and craft: perspective, visuals, audio and editing are distinctive and competent.
10. Compliance and trust: separate hard gate, not offset by a high average score.

A strong overall average never overrides a weak payoff or deceptive opening. Scores are editorial aids, NOT estimates of algorithmic reach or probability of virality.

## Editor checklist
- [ ] I can name the exact moment in which the piece fulfills its opening promise.
- [ ] An unfamiliar person can understand the point from opening image/text/audio.
- [ ] Every beat offers a new reason to stay; no shameless padding.
- [ ] If the CTA were removed, this content still satisfies.
- [ ] A save, share, comment, rewatch or follow would be an authentic response, not forced.
- [ ] It sounds and feels like this property's approved identity.
- [ ] Data/facts, permissions, AI portrayal, sponsorship and privacy are reviewed.
- [ ] The primary intended outcome and measurable hypothesis are recorded.
- [ ] A human editor has approved before publishing during pilot.

## Decision
PASS / REVISE / REJECT. Record one-line reasoning and link to content ID. If REVISE, identify the weakest section, specific action and who owns it. Avoid treating generic hook “power words” as a substitute for story quality.
