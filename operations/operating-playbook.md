# Manual-First Editorial Operating Playbook
Version 1.0 | 2026-10-28

## Weekly operating cadence: one-property pilot
**Monday, research and select:** inspect channel results, competitive references, audience questions, comments and trends. Evaluate a small idea backlog through the property identity, audience jobs and creative economics. Choose only viable concepts.
**Tuesday, design:** create briefs, multiple opening candidates when useful, exact progression and payoff. Research sources, permissions, visual and VO requirements.
**Production:** generate/film/render approved assets; assemble each content package; revise against quality standards. Preserve editable files and proper external asset references.
**Before publish:** apply `creative-approval-gate.md`, `compliance.md`, approve resource CTAs and human approval. Publish manually through approved account.
**After publication:** record IDs, platform settings and actual snapshots. Review at consistent windows, study retention and qualitative audience responses. Capture learning with uncertainty.
**End of week:** choose next experiments; distinguish repeatable success from outlier or trend.

Do not force daily output at the expense of genuine creative quality. Define actual capacity, production time and cost from experience.

## Workflow states
`idea -> researching -> brief_ready -> script_ready -> produced -> in_review -> approved -> published -> measured -> learned`.
Rejection may occur at research, production, or review. Record owner, reason and corrective action. Drafting approval is not automatic publishing permission.

## Source-of-truth files
Ideas: property `ideas.csv`; content: one folder per CNT; calendar: `editorial-calendar.csv`; measured outcomes: `../performance/`; shared research: `../research/`; immutable major choices: `../strategy/decisions.md`.

## Pilot human roles
Operator/editor owns brand voice, creative final approval, claims and sponsorship disclosures, account publishing, financial spending and sensitive decisions. AI may research, draft and prepare assets inside authorized boundaries.

## Scaling gates
Automate only steps with testable quality and low irreversible risk. Keep an approval queue for external actions, logging, retries, rollback and failure alerts. Use platform-sanctioned APIs rather than unauthorized account automation.
