# Quality Standards
Consolidated quality requirements; see `creative-approval-gate.md` for checkable release rules and `../strategy/attention-retention-payoff-standard.md` for the creative philosophy.

## Non-negotiable editorial standard
Compelling opening appropriate to viewer and property; meaningful progressive delivery; satisfying promised payoff; no padding; property-specific personality; original contribution; accurate claims and honest representation; sound/visual/caption quality; rights/consent/disclosure compliance.

## Subjective standards requiring human judgment
Authenticity of creator voice, whether a joke lands, whether a visually understated scene is actually beautiful, whether pacing is appropriate, whether the concept is an unoriginal copy, and whether emotional tactics feel manipulative.

## Technical checks
Captions accurate and readable; audio not robotic or clipped; no broken aspect ratio, abrupt unfinished ending, severe artifacts or inconsistent character design; source materials and media permissions traceable; links and promised resources tested.

## Exceptions
An intentionally silent video should not fail an audio-first standard. A luxury visual montage should not need a spoken CTA. A highly technical demonstration may require slower pacing. Use property-specific definitions, not a single viral formula.

## Publication decision
PASS / REVISE / REJECT. Track changes, editor and approval. Significant defects require revision even if an engagement score averages high.
