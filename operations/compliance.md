# Trust, Rights, Platform and Commercial Compliance
Status: working checklist, not legal advice. Last updated 2026-10-28. Verify current rules for every platform, geography and campaign.

## Synthetic identity and content
- A fictional creator must not falsely claim to be an actual human with genuine personal history, credentials, product use, personal endorsements or real encounters.
- Clearly distinguish synthetic/sketched scenes, fictional interviews and reconstructions from factual footage; follow platform-specific AI-content labeling rules.
- No real person's likeness/voice clone without appropriate rights/consent; no misleading celebrity impersonation.
- Maintain consistent distinction between narrative fiction and documented reporting.

## Information and media rights
Verify factual claims; cite primary sources where applicable; preserve context of quotations. Secure appropriate licenses for footage, photographs, music, marks and third-party clips; commentary/fair use is context-dependent, not blanket authorization.
Respect privacy and avoid exposing sensitive individual data.

## Advertising and commerce
Review FTC endorsement guidelines and applicable advertising regulations. Disclose material sponsorship/affiliate relationships clearly, in context, and according to platform tools. No fabricated product tests/testimonials, false scarcity, unsupported medical or financial claims, or disguised paid endorsements.

## Authentic engagement
Do not buy or automate fake likes/follows/views/comments; no coordinated deceptive amplification, prohibited bots, repetitive engagement bait or misleading comment-to-DM funnels. A request to comment for a resource requires a real resource, clear scope, proper consent/privacy handling and platform-compliant delivery.

## Platform and automation constraints
Before any new integration, verify permissioned API scopes, account authorization, rate limits, scheduling rights, AI labeling, scraping restrictions, data retention and platform-specific content monetization rules. No credential scraping or circumventing anti-bot controls.

## Youth and sensitive contexts
Do not construct covert or personalized targeting based on inferred sensitive characteristics. Be especially careful with minors, health and financial topics; seek qualified review when needed.
