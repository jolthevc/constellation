# Audience and Interest Intelligence
This is a shared source of *hypotheses and sourced evidence*, not a prediction about individuals. Segments (SEG) capture viewing context and needs; interests (INT) capture affinity communities that can overlap many segments.

## Workflow
1. Research and date the specific viewing situation and unmet job, then identify actual competitors, sources and examples.
2. Create `segments/<slug>.md` and/or `interests/<slug>.md` from templates. Keep IDs stable and add an index row.
3. Attach demographic descriptors **only where relevant and evidenced**, avoid stereotyping protected groups, and never infer sensitive attributes of individual users.
4. Map psychological jobs to hook families, credible content, expected payoff and reasons to share/save/follow. Distinguish observing a preference from proving its cause.
5. Test via property content and record results; update status and confidence. Keep original research history.
6. Map commercial categories, not presumed purchasing power, to plausible offers with disclosure and legal checks.

`taxonomy.yaml` describes controlled dimensions. `commercial-categories.yaml` defines opportunities rather than permissions to target or sell.

The initial index records are discovery **candidates only**. No implied survey, audience size, purchasing propensity or social-platform preference.
