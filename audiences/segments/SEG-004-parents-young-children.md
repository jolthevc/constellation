# SEG-004: Parents of young children
**Status:** researching | **Evidence grade:** macro primary or observational proxy, **not** direct segment-specific behavior. | **Research:** 2026-10-28.

## What the evidence says (and does not)
Pew 2026 finds 78% of US adults 30-49 use Facebook, 64% Instagram and 91% YouTube. This is a broad age band, not a parent subsample. Deloitte fans research supports cross-format journeys but does not isolate parents.

**Sources:** SRC-011, SRC-014. Exact URLs and denominator definitions in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`.

## Psychological jobs to investigate
Useful routines, time management, lighthearted shared recognition, community and reassurance. Parents differ widely in needs, budgets and interests.

## Creative strategy hypotheses
**Scroll-stop:** A specific common daily challenge observed from legitimate parent creators or a clearly demonstrated household solution. Avoid creating fictitious firsthand child experiences.

**Retention:** Fast credible steps or comedic escalation with empathetic tone. Avoid manipulating worry about children.

**Payoff, engagement and return:** Practical routine / play idea or genuinely funny situation. Save if a checklist or activity is tangible; share as solidarity.

**Candidate formats:** FMT-045;FMT-069;FMT-078;FMT-139;FMT-124. These are tested hypotheses, not a demographic-format performance ranking.

## Commercial adjacency
COM-010 family products, COM-004 kitchen and household, requiring relevant safety/endorsement reviews.

## Validation plan
Collect direct parent-specific media studies and annotated public creator sample, with privacy restrictions around children. Validate format preference and needs before launch.

**Critical knowledge gap:** No parent-specific quantitative audience behavior available in these sources; status remains exploratory.

## Evidence integrity
Do not extrapolate individual audience interests, gender, disposable income, platform frequency, consumer willingness to pay or watch retention from national platform adoption. Public reference posts and first-party results must be logged separately before upgrading status.
