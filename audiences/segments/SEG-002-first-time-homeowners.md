# SEG-002: First-time homeowners and early home projects
**Status:** researching | **Evidence grade:** macro primary or observational proxy, **not** direct segment-specific behavior. | **Research:** 2026-10-28.

## What the evidence says (and does not)
Houzz's self-selected 2026 research of its users, including 10,176 renovating US homeowners, reports a $20,000 median 2025 renovation spend. HIRI's Q1 2026 tracker reports 24% social media as a home-project research channel; search engines 59%. Neither proves a specific young homeowner's preference or measures creator-driven conversion.

**Sources:** SRC-011, SRC-017, SRC-023. Exact URLs and denominator definitions in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`.

## Psychological jobs to investigate
Reducing repair uncertainty, cost mistakes, decision anxiety, and transforming a space with visible progress. Actual need and budget vary enormously.

## Creative strategy hypotheses
**Scroll-stop:** Show authentic damage/problem or a real fixed result; make the stakes concrete without fearmongering. The claim 'I spent $X' requires actual receipts.

**Retention:** Stepwise diagnostic, cost/option comparison, real evidence and final result. Build forward motion through the task, not arbitrary cuts.

**Payoff, engagement and return:** A working fix, accurate cost framework or clear 'hire a qualified pro' boundary. Saving can be natural if useful; shares may be household decision aids.

**Candidate formats:** FMT-045;FMT-048;FMT-050;FMT-053;FMT-089;FMT-139. These are tested hypotheses, not a demographic-format performance ranking.

## Commercial adjacency
COM-001 home improvement; COM-014 home services; potential high intent but no modeled CAC or conversion.

## Validation plan
Audit real professionals and safe tutorials. Compare visual-first fix demonstration versus mistake-warning opener for comparable low-risk topics. Qualitative correctness/safety gate.

**Critical knowledge gap:** Category commercial demand exists but no cohort-specific social appetite or purchase referral rates; unsafe repairs need professional review.

## Evidence integrity
Do not extrapolate individual audience interests, gender, disposable income, platform frequency, consumer willingness to pay or watch retention from national platform adoption. Public reference posts and first-party results must be logged separately before upgrading status.
