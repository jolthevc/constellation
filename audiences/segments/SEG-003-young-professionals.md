# SEG-003: Early-career professionals
**Status:** researching | **Evidence grade:** macro primary or observational proxy, **not** direct segment-specific behavior. | **Research:** 2026-10-28.

## What the evidence says (and does not)
Pew's closest proxy is broad 18-29/30-49 adult platform adoption: YouTube 95%/91%, Instagram 79%/64%, TikTok 65%/45%. These are not career-stage samples. Buffer's format data describe posting-provider customers, not young workers.

**Sources:** SRC-011, SRC-014, SRC-013. Exact URLs and denominator definitions in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`.

## Psychological jobs to investigate
Workplace humor, career mastery, social confidence, useful routines, recognition of struggles without negative stereotypes.

## Creative strategy hypotheses
**Scroll-stop:** Highly specific real office/work moment or an evidence-based counterintuitive skill tip; not generic motivational claims.

**Retention:** Scene escalation in comedy, or demonstrable before/after explanation and concrete examples in utility.

**Payoff, engagement and return:** An honest laugh, better-informed career decision or usable technique. Shares for peer recognition; saves for checklists.

**Candidate formats:** FMT-002;FMT-040;FMT-047;FMT-045;FMT-139;FMT-141. These are tested hypotheses, not a demographic-format performance ranking.

## Commercial adjacency
COM-008 education; COM-009 software; inappropriate to invent career outcomes.

## Validation plan
Study peer-workplace humor and career advice creators. A/B compare original relatable sketch and practical visual guide at similar production quality; monitor follow intent and meaningful comments.

**Critical knowledge gap:** No cited career-professional-specific social motivation distribution; validate before claiming a particular 'young professional' psychology.

## Evidence integrity
Do not extrapolate individual audience interests, gender, disposable income, platform frequency, consumer willingness to pay or watch retention from national platform adoption. Public reference posts and first-party results must be logged separately before upgrading status.
