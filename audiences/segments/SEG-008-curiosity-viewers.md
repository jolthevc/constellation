# SEG-008: Broad curiosity-driven documentary viewers
**Status:** researching | **Evidence grade:** macro primary or observational proxy, **not** direct segment-specific behavior. | **Research:** 2026-10-28.

## What the evidence says (and does not)
Pew 2026 finds YouTube used by 84% of US adults and 34% regularly get news from YouTube; news is only a proxy for informational viewing. Deloitte 2026 fans survey shows multi-platform discovery and continued consumption elsewhere. YouTube says Shorts favor no particular format and consider chose-to-view and satisfaction.

**Sources:** SRC-011, SRC-014, SRC-024, SRC-025. Exact URLs and denominator definitions in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`.

## Psychological jobs to investigate
Surprise, understanding, coherent narrative, cultural relevance and satisfying explanation. These are creative hypotheses requiring actual viewer testing.

## Creative strategy hypotheses
**Scroll-stop:** Specific verified unexpected fact or vivid original question, immediately legible graphics or strong first image.

**Retention:** Evidence sequence, narrative escalation, reversals grounded in fact, and regular mini-payoffs rather than sustained teasing.

**Payoff, engagement and return:** Specific documented explanation, emotional resonance or genuine wonder. Share-worthy revelation if true; follow for reliable originality.

**Candidate formats:** FMT-031;FMT-034;FMT-064;FMT-089;FMT-092;FMT-135;FMT-136. These are tested hypotheses, not a demographic-format performance ranking.

## Commercial adjacency
COM-012 entertainment and media, COM-008 education, COM-009 related tools when fit is earned; likely reach-first before commerce.

## Validation plan
Audit 10 independent documentary/explainer brands including low performers. Pilot 3-5 repeatable story structures, compare hook hold and follow per view, assess footage rights and sourcing expense.

**Critical knowledge gap:** No quantitative source yet measures short-form curiosity genre-specific demand or our ability to produce at acceptable quality/cost.

## Evidence integrity
Do not extrapolate individual audience interests, gender, disposable income, platform frequency, consumer willingness to pay or watch retention from national platform adoption. Public reference posts and first-party results must be logged separately before upgrading status.
