# SEG-006: Home services and trades professionals
**Status:** researching | **Evidence grade:** macro primary or observational proxy, **not** direct segment-specific behavior. | **Research:** 2026-10-28.

## What the evidence says (and does not)
Pew provides platform usage by demographic but **not occupational subgroup**. HIRI/Houzz describe homeowners and their projects, not tradespeople's media usage. There is no trade-worker sample in the current source set.

**Sources:** SRC-011, SRC-023, SRC-017. Exact URLs and denominator definitions in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`.

## Psychological jobs to investigate
Mastery, practitioner humor, respect for skill, tool knowledge and trade community recognition. All are hypotheses pending occupational interviews or creator audit.

## Creative strategy hypotheses
**Scroll-stop:** A technically accurate unusual jobsite detail or genuine professional mistake/solution. No fake on-site filming or unsafe instruction.

**Retention:** Technical proof, realistic process, craft detail or authentic community joke with clear payoff.

**Payoff, engagement and return:** Useful field insight, accurate tool comparison or shared professional humor. Strong potential commerce adjacency but not demonstrated audience loyalty.

**Candidate formats:** FMT-026;FMT-050;FMT-078;FMT-139;FMT-081. These are tested hypotheses, not a demographic-format performance ranking.

## Commercial adjacency
COM-001 supplies/tools; COM-014 services; professional safety/claims review required.

## Validation plan
Prioritize practitioner interviews or real creator sample before choosing a synthetic format. Test whether faceless editorial can earn trust without pretending professional experience.

**Critical knowledge gap:** No defensible quantitative niche reach, creative format fit, purchase intent or platform age mix. Research priority high, confidence low.

## Evidence integrity
Do not extrapolate individual audience interests, gender, disposable income, platform frequency, consumer willingness to pay or watch retention from national platform adoption. Public reference posts and first-party results must be logged separately before upgrading status.
