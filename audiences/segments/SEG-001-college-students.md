# SEG-001: College students and early adult life
**Status:** researching | **Evidence grade:** macro primary or observational proxy, **not** direct segment-specific behavior. | **Research:** 2026-10-28.

## What the evidence says (and does not)
Pew finds 79% of US adults 18-29 ever use Instagram, 65% TikTok and 95% YouTube in 2026. These are **all young adults**, not a college-student sample. Deloitte reports a high share of Gen Z *fans* discover media on social (73%); this does not measure a particular campus audience. Pew teen work applies to minors and cannot be generalized to college adults.

**Sources:** SRC-011, SRC-014, SRC-019, SRC-027. Exact URLs and denominator definitions in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`.

## Psychological jobs to investigate
Identity, friendship, status, transitions, humor, finding one's people, practical campus life. These are hypotheses to check at particular institutions, not universal traits.

## Creative strategy hypotheses
**Scroll-stop:** Recognition of a genuinely observed student routine; an original comic contradiction; specific useful time/budget insight. Avoid pretending a generated student is real or inventing interviews.

**Retention:** Escalating campus scenario, revealing a concrete fact, before/after or short narrative with early reward.

**Payoff, engagement and return:** Laugh of recognition or useful actionable tip. Share for in-group humor; save only for genuinely useful information. Follow when the property consistently interprets a school/culture, not because of generic trend reposts.

**Candidate formats:** FMT-002;FMT-040;FMT-139;FMT-135;FMT-124. These are tested hypotheses, not a demographic-format performance ranking.

## Commercial adjacency
COM-008 education; COM-009 software/apps; COM-003 apparel only where legally appropriate and original. Youth/college age distinction and school marketing policies matter.

## Validation plan
Audit 10 adult-student-focused creators and campus editorial accounts. Test humor versus practical creator ideas on adult 18+ sample; monitor real non-follower discovery, shares and follows. Do not infer age from comments.

**Critical knowledge gap:** No survey here specifically maps format preferences of US college students, or buyer conversion.

## Evidence integrity
Do not extrapolate individual audience interests, gender, disposable income, platform frequency, consumer willingness to pay or watch retention from national platform adoption. Public reference posts and first-party results must be logged separately before upgrading status.
