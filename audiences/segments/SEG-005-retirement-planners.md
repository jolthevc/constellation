# SEG-005: Retirement planners and older adults
**Status:** researching | **Evidence grade:** macro primary or observational proxy, **not** direct segment-specific behavior. | **Research:** 2026-10-28.

## What the evidence says (and does not)
Pew 2026: adults ages 50-64: YouTube 85%, Facebook 71%; ages 65+: YouTube 65%, Facebook 59%. AARP surveyed US adults 50+ and reported about 90% use social media and 90% own smartphones in 2025. Edison says monthly online audio reach for people 55+ rose to 70% in 2026. These frames must not be conflated.

**Sources:** SRC-011, SRC-012, SRC-016. Exact URLs and denominator definitions in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`.

## Psychological jobs to investigate
Trusted practical help, informed decision, independence and interesting life discoveries; avoid patronizing age-based assumptions.

## Creative strategy hypotheses
**Scroll-stop:** Immediately useful genuine insight, appealing original story or relatable problem with verified answer; visual and audio clarity over hyperactive 'viral' style.

**Retention:** Calm progressive explanation and trustworthy sourcing, easy-to-follow demonstration, pacing chosen for content not age stereotype.

**Payoff, engagement and return:** Knowledge that can be applied or an engaging original story with a concrete resolution. Save/share for future utility or family discussions.

**Candidate formats:** FMT-007;FMT-045;FMT-060;FMT-089;FMT-139;FMT-149. These are tested hypotheses, not a demographic-format performance ranking.

## Commercial adjacency
COM-008 learning, COM-009 software, COM-005 travel, high scrutiny on finance/health claims; do not assume purchasing ability.

## Validation plan
Research older-adult creators, test adult viewer accessibility and comprehension with voluntary participants; compare simple graphic-led versus trusted expert format.

**Critical knowledge gap:** AARP is older adult tech, not verified retirement-planner media preference; niche-specific demand unmeasured.

## Evidence integrity
Do not extrapolate individual audience interests, gender, disposable income, platform frequency, consumer willingness to pay or watch retention from national platform adoption. Public reference posts and first-party results must be logged separately before upgrading status.
