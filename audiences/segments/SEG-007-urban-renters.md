# SEG-007: Urban renters and space-conscious living
**Status:** researching | **Evidence grade:** macro primary or observational proxy, **not** direct segment-specific behavior. | **Research:** 2026-10-28.

## What the evidence says (and does not)
Pew 2026 reports YouTube 87%, Instagram 58% and TikTok 43% among US urban adults, but that is *not* a renter sample. Buffer's Instagram formats analysis finds carousels more engagement per reach in its managed-post sample, which is not evidence specific to renters or home decor.

**Sources:** SRC-011, SRC-013, SRC-022. Exact URLs and denominator definitions in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`.

## Psychological jobs to investigate
Transforming limited space, budget utility, aesthetic inspiration, identity expression and clever constraints; hypotheses, not demographic guarantees.

## Creative strategy hypotheses
**Scroll-stop:** A visually striking before/after small space, genuine low-cost fix or clearly beautiful room image.

**Retention:** Progressive tour, annotated layout decisions and specific practical tradeoffs.

**Payoff, engagement and return:** An actual sourcing guide or transformation reveal; natural saves for future reference and shares with roommates.

**Candidate formats:** FMT-073;FMT-100;FMT-139;FMT-135;FMT-051. These are tested hypotheses, not a demographic-format performance ranking.

## Commercial adjacency
COM-001 household tools; COM-003 furnishings and decor adjacent, not necessarily clothing; COM-014 specialist services if relevant.

## Validation plan
Audit renter-specific design accounts versus general interiors creators, compare carousel guide and high-quality short reveal, measure saves per qualified reach.

**Critical knowledge gap:** No quantified renter audience, content preference or conversion from this macro dataset.

## Evidence integrity
Do not extrapolate individual audience interests, gender, disposable income, platform frequency, consumer willingness to pay or watch retention from national platform adoption. Public reference posts and first-party results must be logged separately before upgrading status.
