# INT-010: Fashion and personal style
**Status:** researching, evidence-informed hypotheses | **Review date:** 2026-10-28

## Quantitative context and boundaries
Pew 2026 (SRC-011) finds Instagram ever-use 79% ages 18-29, 64% ages 30-49, but no fashion-affinity measurement. Buffer format data (SRC-013) are broad. No fashion product conversion evidence collected.

Full source links, dates, populations and limitations are in `../../research/sources.csv` and `../../research/datasets/evidence-points-2026.csv`. **No evidence here establishes the number of viewers in this precise interest community, their social media format preferences or expected monetization.**

## Psychological job hypotheses
Taste expression, identity signaling, aspiration, confidence and discovery; aesthetics can lead without speech.
Study subcommunities separately. Do not assign universal preferences based on demographic labels.

## Original creative proposition
Strong styling/visual first frame -> coherent selections and variation -> delightful full look or real sourcing information. Avoid loud 'must have' pressure.

**Possible formats:** FMT-072;FMT-100;FMT-139;FMT-118;FMT-135. Research and test rather than assume performance.

## Share/save/follow design
- **Share:** a true surprising insight or socially recognizable in-group idea.
- **Save:** only when content supplies real future reference, actionable steps or an inspiring collection.
- **Follow:** consistent voice, reliable creative quality and recurring series with meaningful standalone payoff.
- **CTAs:** optional, specific and natural; no empty follow, keyword or comment bait.
